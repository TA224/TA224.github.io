Privacy Policy for Site Block

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

Overview

Site Block: Block distractions and Boost productivity ("we", "our", or "the extension") helps you improve your focus by blocking distracting websites, optionally synchronizing your focus modes with Google Calendar, and optionally sending accountability alerts to a Discord channel that you choose. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information Site Block accesses, how it is used, and your control over your data.

Data Collection and Storage

Local Data Only

All data collected by Site Block is stored locally on your device using Chrome’s storage.local and storage.session APIs. No data is ever transmitted to servers owned or operated by us. Data leaves your device only in two optional cases that you turn on yourself: Google Calendar sync (data goes to Google) and accountability alerts (message text goes to a Discord webhook that you provide). Both are described below.

Site Block stores locally:

  • Blocked sites list – Websites you choose to block or allow
  • Focus mode schedules – Your configured focus sessions
  • Timer settings – Your preferred focus and break durations
  • Strict mode passcode – Optional passcode to prevent disabling Site Block
  • Mode definitions – Your focus blocks (recurring or one‑off)
  • Excluded dates and exception events – Used for recurring mode management
  • (Optional) Google Calendar event mapping – A local map between your modes and Google Calendar events, used only for sync
  • (Optional) Accountability settings – Your Discord webhook URL, the send chance, which alerts are switched on and their timings, and the result of the most recent alert (when it happened, what triggered it, the dice roll and whether a message was sent). The webhook URL works like a password for posting to that Discord channel. It is stored only in this browser profile’s extension storage, and the only place it is ever used is to send an alert to Discord.
  • (Optional) Accountability activity data – Only while accountability alerts are switched on: a short‑lived log of recently opened web pages (their addresses) and the times of recent clicks, scrolls or key presses on them, kept in session storage. It is cleared when you close the browser, and entries are dropped after about three hours. Small per‑event counters (for example, minutes spent away from an event’s allowed sites) are kept for up to three days so the same event does not trigger twice, and the time of the last alert for each blocked site (by hostname) is kept for up to a day so refreshing a page does not trigger repeated alerts.

Google Calendar Integration (Optional)

If you choose to enable Google Calendar synchronization, Site Block uses Google’s OAuth 2.0 to request permission to read and write events in a dedicated calendar (named “Site Block”) or your primary calendar (depending on your preference). This is done via Chrome’s secure chrome.identity API.

What is accessed:

  • The extension can read and write events in your selected Google Calendar to keep your focus modes and calendar events in sync.
  • It only accesses the calendar you explicitly authorize during the OAuth consent flow.
  • To keep modes in sync, the events it writes contain your mode names, schedules and site lists, and the type of each mode (for example, block or whitelist).

How data is handled:

  • No calendar data is sent to any third‑party server. All communication is directly between your browser and Google’s APIs.
  • The OAuth token is managed by Chrome and is never stored in extension storage; it is only used temporarily to make API calls.
  • You can revoke the extension’s access at any time via your Google Account’s “Third‑party apps with account access” page.
  • Site Block’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we do not do:

  • We do not store, log, or transmit your calendar events, credentials, or any personal information to our own servers.
  • We do not share your calendar data with any third party.

Accountability Alerts (Optional)

This feature is off by default. If you switch it on and enter a Discord webhook URL, Site Block can post short messages to that Discord channel when you slip, for example when a whitelist event starts and you have not opened or used an allowed site, when you spend a set amount of time away from an event’s allowed sites, or when you open a blocked site. For each of these moments the extension rolls a virtual die on your device, and the roll decides whether a message is sent.

What is sent:

  • Only the text of the message. It can include the name you gave an event, the hostname of an allowed or blocked site (for example “reddit.com”, never the full address or path), the time, and a number of minutes.
  • The message is sent directly from your browser to Discord’s servers (discord.com). We do not receive a copy and there is no server of ours in between.
  • Discord’s Privacy Policy applies to messages once they reach Discord, and anyone who can see that channel can read them. You are responsible for choosing a channel you are comfortable sharing with.

What is never sent:

  • Full web addresses, page content, search terms, or your browsing history.
  • What you type, the keys you press, mouse positions, or anything in forms.

How activity is detected on your device:

  • To tell whether you are actually using an allowed site (and not just have it open), Site Block includes a small script that runs on web pages and notes when you click, scroll or press a key.
  • It reports only the kind of action (“click”, “scroll” or “key”), at most once every 15 seconds per page, and only while the page is visible. It does not read page content or the keys you press.
  • The script is present on pages whether or not alerts are on, but the extension ignores and does not store what it reports unless accountability alerts are switched on. These signals stay on your device and are used only to decide whether an alert applies.

Your control:

  • Switch the feature off, or remove the webhook URL, in the Site Block settings page at any time. Nothing is detected or sent after that.
  • You can also delete the webhook in Discord, which makes the URL stop working.
  • Messages that were already sent stay in your Discord channel until you delete them there.

What Site Block Accesses

To provide its core functionality and optional features, Site Block requires these Chrome permissions:

Permission Purpose
All websites / Host Access To detect when you visit a blocked site and apply rules, and to run the activity‑detection script (click, scroll and key press type only) used by the optional accountability alerts
Active Tab To temporarily access the active tab upon user interaction
Tabs and browsing activity To check open tabs against your block list and, if you enable accountability alerts, to know which tab you are using so time away from allowed sites can be measured
Web Navigation To monitor and track navigation events
Declarative Net Request To intercept and block attempts to load restricted sites
Notifications To display focus reminders and alerts
Storage To save your settings locally
Identity (Optional) To authenticate with Google and access account email for Calendar sync
Alarms To schedule periodic background tasks and timers

How Your Data Is Used

Your locally stored data is used only to:

  • Enforce your site blocking preferences
  • Activate focus mode according to your schedule
  • Maintain your timer settings
  • Verify your strict mode passcode when needed
  • Manage recurring focus modes and exceptions
  • (If enabled) Synchronize your modes with Google Calendar
  • (If enabled) Decide whether an accountability alert applies and post it to your Discord webhook

Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Transmit your data to servers owned or operated by us
  • Transmit your data to anyone else, except for the two optional features you control: Google Calendar sync, which sends data only to Google’s APIs as per your authorization, and accountability alerts, which send message text only to the Discord webhook you provide
  • Use analytics or tracking tools
  • Share data with advertisers or third parties
  • Collect or store your browsing history beyond checking pages against your block list. If you enable accountability alerts, a short‑lived log of recently opened pages is kept on your device as described above; it is never uploaded, and alert messages contain at most a site’s hostname

Your Rights and Control

You have full control:

  • View/Modify – All settings are accessible in the Site Block popup and settings page.
  • Delete – Uninstalling Site Block automatically removes all locally stored data.
  • Revoke Google Calendar access – Go to your Google Account → Security → Third‑party apps with account access → Remove Site Block.
  • Stop accountability alerts – Switch the feature off or clear the webhook URL in the settings page; you can also delete the webhook in Discord. Delete any messages already sent from your Discord channel.
  • Clear all settings – Use the “Reset” option within the extension interface.

Children’s Privacy
